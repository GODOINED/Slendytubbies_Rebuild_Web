Slendytubbies Rebuild

A complete reimagining of the classic — with working multiplayer through Steam. Collect custard, hide from monsters and survive together with your friends. Or become one of them.

The project recreates the atmosphere and mechanics of Slendytubbies III, but with an improved networking layer, new game modes and an expanded roster of enemies. Familiar maps, dark locations, and a deadly threat that only gets worse with every update.

Game Modes

Classic (CO-OP)

The classic custard-collecting mode against the clock. Players team up, explore the map and find every custard portion while a monster stalks them. Victory comes only through cooperation.

Versus

One player becomes the monster and hunts the survivors. The rest must collect all custard before the timer runs out. Tension grows every second — because now the enemy thinks like you.

Classic+

A harder version of classic. Collecting custard is not enough — you must find a key and open the door, start the generator to restore power, or complete another objective. Depends on the map.

RolePlay

A free mode with no rules and no objectives. Explore locations, talk to other players, act out scenes and create your own stories within the Slendytubbies universe.

Enemies

In Rebuild you won't face only the classic Tinky Winky. Other infected Teletubbies hunt on different maps too.

Tinky Winky

The main antagonist of the series. Chases players, guards the nearest custard and never forgives mistakes.

Dipsy

A blind mutant who hunts by sound. He cannot see players at all, but hears every step. Fast and extremely aggressive — on maps like Custard Facility, getting close is fatal.

Laa-Laa

Tall and blind, yet deadly. Hunts by sound and appears on the night versions of the maps.

Po

The red Teletubby who became the final boss. Slower than the others, but deals devastating damage.

Steam Multiplayer

At the core of Rebuild lies a working online game. Create a lobby, invite your friends, pick a map and a mode. All players see each other, monsters follow a shared logic, and progress syncs in real time.
